Why Choose Us

Por Qué Elegirnos

Two Decades of Expat Legal Experience

For over 20 years, we've been the trusted legal partner for Americans and Canadians making Puerto Vallarta and Bahía de Banderas their home.

With 99% of our practice dedicated to expat clients, we understand your unique needs, concerns, and the challenges of navigating the Mexican legal system as a foreigner.

Whether you're buying your dream beachfront condo, setting up residency, or ensuring your estate is protected, we provide clear guidance in English every step of the way.
